Checks

  • Frozen first. Numbers and tolerances tagged claims-frozen-cam4-2024-hpd before any analysis.
  • Re-run. No authors' code; one script written from the methods. Runtime 2 s.
  • Audited. Re-run three times, identical. Same estimates by an independent route. No verdict changed.
What the paper says to do
  1. Load sheet 1 ('meta_data_22.02.14_analysis') of updated_extracted_data_HPD_22.08.29.xlsx: 34 rows, one per study. Columns: study, year, hpd (events), total (patients), definition (A/B/C), histology (AGC/HCC/NSCLC/RCC/'mixed or other'). Do not exclude anything; all 34 studies enter the main analysis (the paper reports 558 HPD / 4117 patients).
  2. Pooled incidence (C1, C2): random-effects meta-analysis of single proportions as a generalized linear mixed model (binomial-normal, logit link), estimated by maximum likelihood with adaptive Gauss-Hermite quadrature. In R: meta::metaprop(event = hpd, n = total, sm = 'PLOGIT', method = 'GLMM', method.tau = 'ML', and t-distribution (Hartung-Knapp) CI: hakn = TRUE / method.random.ci = 'HK' depending on meta version), which calls metafor::rma.glmm(measure = 'PLO', xi = hpd, ni = total, method = 'ML', test = 't'). Back-transform the pooled logit and its 95% CI to percent. Also report Cochran Q (with p), I2 and the 95% prediction interval.
  3. Zero-event study: Zheng et al 2021 (hpd = 0, total = 30, RCC). GLMM needs no continuity correction; keep the study as is.
  4. Subgroups (context, not headline): fit the same GLMM pooling within each definition (A/B/C) and each histology. Univariable meta-regression on definition gives p = 0.17 and on histology p = 0.004.
  5. Multivariable meta-regression (C3–C6): mixed-effects logistic meta-regression rma.glmm(measure = 'PLO', xi = hpd, ni = total, mods = ~ histology + definition, method = 'ML', test = 't') with reference levels histology = 'RCC' and definition = 'C' (relevel factors). Report exp(coef) as adjusted ORs with 95% CI and two-sided p for AGC, HCC, NSCLC, 'mixed or other' (vs RCC) and A, B (vs C). Equivalent: meta::metareg on the metaprop GLMM object with formula ~ histology + definition.
  6. Exponentiate the coefficients and CI bounds. Round ORs to 2 decimals and incidence to 1 decimal percent, as the paper prints them.
What the paper leaves unclear
  • Internal inconsistency between the reported ORs, CIs and p-values in Table 1 / Abstract: back-calculating a Wald SE from the printed tumor-type CIs gives p of about 0.004 (AGC), 0.009 (HCC), 0.011 (NSCLC) and 0.035 (mixed/other). The printed values are <0.001, 0.006, 0.005 and 0.018, and with a t-based CI the implied p would be even larger. The definition rows (A: 0.335, B: 0.025) are consistent with their CIs. Either the CIs and p-values come from different tests (e.g., CI t-based, p from a z or likelihood-ratio test), or the p-values were taken from another model (the univariable p-values in the Results text are AGC <0.001, NSCLC 0.002, HCC 0.002, mixed 0.005, which do not match either). The p-values for C3–C5 should therefore not be expected to match the CIs exactly.
  • Which Q statistic: for a GLMM, metafor/meta report both a Wald-type and a likelihood-ratio heterogeneity test (QE.Wld, QE.LRT), and meta's print also gives an inverse-variance Q. The paper does not say which one is Q = 119.32.
  • The t-distribution CI is stated ('95% CIs were estimated using the Student's t-distribution'), but it is unclear whether the t-adjustment (test = 't') was also applied to the meta-regression CIs and p-values in Table 1, and what df was used.
  • Meta-regression reference levels and coding are taken from Table 1 (RCC, definition C). 'Mixed or other' in the data pools mixed cohorts (7) with HNSCC (2), melanoma (2) and sarcoma (1), following the rule 'tumor types were considered as subgroups only when more than two studies were available'. The data file already encodes this, but the paper does not say so explicitly.
  • The method text says 'adaptive Gaussian hermite quadrature', but the number of quadrature points (nAGQ, passed to lme4::glmer by rma.glmm for measure='PLO') and the optimizer are not stated. These can shift ORs in the second decimal.
  • Package versions for metafor and meta are not given. meta's argument names for the t-based/HK CI changed across versions (hakn vs method.random.ci).
  • Sheet names say '22.02.14' while the file name says '22.08.29'; sheet 1 and sheet 2 list the same 34 studies in slightly different order. It is assumed sheet 1 was the analysis input.
  • The Results text gives the definition B subgroup CI upper bound as '27.12%' (2 decimals), while the other bounds use 1 decimal. This is a minor inconsistency in printing precision.
  • Methods mention 'univariable and multivariable meta-regression'. The Abstract wording ('risk of developing HPD was higher') presents odds ratios as risk, which is a wording issue only.
  • Studies with overlapping cohorts and one two-cohort study (UC/RCC, the RCC arm kept) were resolved before deposit; the deposit cannot verify these choices.
